# Elkaza commercial activation checklist

Owner review only. This checklist records matters to confirm before activating a commercial offer or business contact channel. It does not assert that any item is already complete and is not legal or tax advice.

## Austrian business scope

- [ ] Confirm the applicable Austrian `Gewerbe` scope with a qualified authority or adviser.
- [ ] Confirm which planned IT activities are permitted within that scope.
- [ ] Remove or defer any service description that falls outside the confirmed scope.

## Provider identification and contact

- [ ] Complete the Impressum/provider identification with verified required details.
- [ ] Create a dedicated business email/contact channel.
- [ ] Decide who receives, stores, and answers commercial inquiries.
- [ ] Recheck domain, hosting, and provider disclosures.

## Privacy and contact processing

- [ ] Document the purpose and legal basis for contact-data processing.
- [ ] Define retention and deletion rules for inquiries.
- [ ] Update the privacy notice before enabling a form, analytics, CRM, or marketing tool.
- [ ] Verify processors and data-processing agreements where applicable.

## Contracts and delivery boundaries

- [ ] Prepare written scope, deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, and exclusions.
- [ ] Define acceptance and change-control procedures.
- [ ] Confirm customer responsibilities, access requirements, and backup responsibilities.
- [ ] Review liability limitations and contractual terms with a qualified adviser.

## Insurance, invoicing, and tax

- [ ] Assess appropriate professional/business liability insurance.
- [ ] Confirm invoicing requirements, bookkeeping, VAT, and tax setup.
- [ ] Confirm payment terms and record-keeping procedures.
- [ ] Replace all illustrative package prices only with reviewed, commercially viable pricing.

## Managed-service readiness (only if later activated)

- [ ] Define the exact supported systems and exclusions.
- [ ] Establish monitoring ownership, tooling, alert routing, and escalation procedures.
- [ ] Define support hours, response targets, maintenance windows, and incident classifications contractually.
- [ ] Validate staffing/capacity, holiday coverage, security controls, and business continuity.
- [ ] Prepare and review an SLA model; do not advertise 24/7 coverage or guaranteed reaction times before it exists.

- [ ] Test German and English pages, contact processing, legal links, accessibility, and production monitoring.
- [ ] Record the owner approval date and the evidence used for each activated claim.
